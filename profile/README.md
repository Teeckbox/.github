This project aims to provide a modern, flexible and self-hostable platform for managing **cases, processes and workflows** in a structured and transparent way.

The core idea is to combine **case management, configurable workflows, business rules and AI-assisted context awareness** without forcing organizations into rigid predefined processes.

Key objectives include:

- **Case-centric management** — keep all information, variables, tasks, conditions and activities related to a case in one coherent context.
- **Configurable templates** — define reusable case templates, including variables, fields, tasks, conditions and their dependencies.
- **Explicit dependencies** — model relationships between variables, conditions and tasks so that the system can explain why a task is available, blocked or requires specific information.
- **Flexible workflows** — support real-world processes that may evolve dynamically rather than relying exclusively on rigid linear workflows.
- **AI-assisted case understanding** — use LLMs to generate concise, contextual descriptions of the current state of a case and help users understand what requires attention.
- **Self-hosting and data ownership** — provide organizations with full control over their data and deployment.
- **Modern user experience** — offer an intuitive, responsive interface suitable for both everyday users and process administrators.
- **Extensibility** — provide a clean architecture and APIs that allow integrations and future extensions without coupling the core system to a specific technology or provider.

The project is designed to be **general-purpose**, allowing it to be adapted to different business processes and organizational needs rather than targeting a single industry or workflow.
